About Jierui Semiconductor

Shenzhen Jierui Semiconductor Co., Ltd. was established in 2025.


We specialize in semiconductor memory products, including LPDDR4, LPDDR4X, LPDDR5, DDR3, DDR4 and DDR5 solutions.


Through reliable supply chain resources, we provide professional memory solutions for consumer electronics customers worldwide.

深圳市杰銳半導體科技有限公司成立於2025年。
